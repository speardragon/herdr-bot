# Agent-kind icons

`claude.svg`, `codex.svg` (OpenAI mark, used for Codex), `gemini.svg` (Google Gemini mark), and
`opencode.svg` are glyphs from the [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) project
(https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), fetched from
`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@latest/icons/<slug>.svg` on 2026-09-09.

Simple Icons releases the glyph artwork under CC0 1.0 (public domain); the brand names and marks
themselves remain trademarks of their respective owners (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google). No icon for
Grok/xAI exists in Simple Icons at the time of writing, so the New Bot dialog falls back to a plain
monogram badge for that kind.
